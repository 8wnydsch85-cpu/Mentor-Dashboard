        Tecvid, from the ground up

        Fifteen lessons, one a week, each building on the last: sounds first, then lengths, then the nun and mim rules, then the finishing rules. Every lesson uses the same five-step format and practises in surahs the group already knows. A rule counts as mastered only when a student applies it correctly in a passage they have not just drilled.

        Lessons

        Open a lesson for the rule, how to teach it, practice ayahs and the check.

        How each lesson runs

        About 40 minutes
        1. Review · 5 minEach student reads one line using last week's rule. Fix it before moving on.
        2. Explain · 8 minName the rule, show the letters on the board, and say why it exists: the Qur'an is recited as it was taught to the Prophet ﷺ.
        3. Model and repeat · 10 minYou read the example; they repeat together, then one by one. Exaggerate slightly at first.
        4. Find it · 10 minOpen a short surah. Students hunt for every place the rule occurs and read each aloud.
        5. Check · 7 minEach student reads the check passage alone. Mark them learning or mastered here before you leave.

        Teaching notes

        • Correct one thing at a time. If a student makes three mistakes, fix the one from this week's rule and note the rest.
        • Praise the specific thing done right: "Your ghunna on إِنَّ was the full length."
        • Have them record a voice note of the check passage each week. Listening to themselves fixes more than being told.
        • Pair a stronger reader with one who is behind for the Find it step.
        • Never move a student to mastered on the day the rule was taught. Confirm it the following week.
        • If most of the group is still learning a rule, repeat the lesson rather than starting the next.
